    Questions, answered

    Can’t find yours? Write to us and a real person will reply.

    Who can open a business account?

    Registered companies and sole traders. The list of supported countries and business types is shown when you apply.

    Is iPay a bank?

    No. iPay is a technology company. Accounts, wallets and cards are provided through licensed partners in the European Economic Area.

    Which currencies can we hold?

    US dollars and euros in your accounts, USDT and USDC in the wallet. You can exchange between them at the rate shown before you confirm.

    Can each team have its own card?

    Yes. Issue cards for your teams, set a limit on each one and freeze any card in a tap.

    What documents do we need?

    Usually a certificate of registration, details of directors and shareholders, and an ID for each of them. We’ll show the exact list for your country.

    Where do we manage the account?

    In the browser: balances, transfers, cards and history in one dashboard.
