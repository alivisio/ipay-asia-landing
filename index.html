      Which countries can I use iPay in?

      Pay with your iPay card in shops, online and at ATMs in Malaysia and across Asia — Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam, the Philippines, Japan, South Korea and Hong Kong — and in 180+ countries worldwide. Apple Pay and Google Pay work too.

      Is iPay a bank?

      No. iPay is a licensed fintech. Cards, wallets and e-money are provided through licensed partners in the European Economic Area, and your funds are safeguarded.

      Which currencies can I hold?

      You can hold US dollars, euros and popular crypto. When you pay in another currency, like ringgit or baht, it’s converted at the rate shown in the app.

      How do I open an account?

      Download the app, sign up with your email and verify your identity with an ID document and a selfie. It usually takes about 3 minutes.

      Are there limits?

      You can send up to €5,000 a day and €100,000 a month. Set lower limits for each card in the app.
